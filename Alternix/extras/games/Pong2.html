How to Play

Two-player touchscreen: Player 1 drags up/down in the left touch area; Player 2 uses the right touch area. Both fingers work at the same time. You can also drag directly within your half of the playfield. The outside touch areas move paddles without obscuring the ball.

Keyboard: Player 1 uses W / S; Player 2 uses ↑ / ↓. Two connected gamepads can also use their left sticks or D-pads.

Rules: Get the ball past the other paddle to score. Paddle contact angle changes the return. First player to the selected score wins (by two, if enabled).

Random effects: Both paddles flash for two seconds before one of four temporary effects starts. Multiball adds two balls (the first ball past either paddle scores the point), Sticky catches and automatically releases balls, Speedup makes all balls faster, and Shrink makes both paddles shorter. Effects last eight seconds and are fair to both players. You can switch them off in Settings.

Game controls: Menu button or Esc / P pauses. Fullscreen button or F toggles fullscreen.
